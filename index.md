**AFM-IR** combines atomic force microscopy with infrared spectroscopy to record
chemical information at the nanoscale — far below the diffraction limit of
conventional IR microscopy.

This site collects practical information about the technique: how it works,
what to watch out for when interpreting data, key papers, software and labs.
