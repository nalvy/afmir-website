## How AFM-IR works

A pulsed, tunable infrared laser illuminates the sample under an AFM tip.
When the laser is tuned to an absorption band, the sample heats up and expands
slightly. The AFM cantilever picks up this tiny, fast expansion as an
oscillation. Recording the oscillation amplitude while sweeping the laser
wavelength gives a local IR spectrum; scanning at a fixed wavelength gives a
chemical map.
