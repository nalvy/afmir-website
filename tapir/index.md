## What it does

- **Import** spectra and images from AFM-IR instruments and common formats
- **Correct** spectra — baseline, smoothing, spectral range, step discontinuities
- **Fit** peaks and inspect results
- **Image** operations — subtraction, normalisation and other image maths
- **Hyperspectral** maps — *in development*
